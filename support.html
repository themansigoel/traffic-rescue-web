Playing the game

Tap a vehicle to move it along its available route. Check what is blocking it before you tap: a blocked vehicle tap also uses a move. Start at Level 1 and solve puzzles to unlock more. Version 1 has five worlds and 64 levels.

In Traffic Lights, wait for green before moving. In Construction City, moving the right construction vehicle opens a blocked lane. Use replay to try a fresh plan.

School bus pickups

  1. Clear every other traffic vehicle first.
  2. Tap the bus to reach Stop 1. Wait while the children board automatically.
  3. Tap again to reach the next numbered stop. Repeat in order.
  4. After all pickups, tap once more to reach school and drop everyone off.

The bus has space for all the waiting children. You do not need to tap the children, and the bus makes one final school visit.

Hints and advertisements

Every level has two free hints. Free hint usage is saved; replaying does not refill it. Extra hints are optional rewarded ads, with no coin charge. Skipping an ad or a failed ad does not earn a hint. If no hint is available, the game does not consume one.

An interstitial may appear after every third completed level. Remove Ads disables interstitials; rewarded ads remain optional. If an ad is unavailable, check your connection and try later.

Saved progress and iCloud

The game saves your progress on your device. Choose Sync in Settings to enable iCloud synchronization. Use the same Apple account on both devices and allow time for changes to propagate. Purchases are restored through the shop, separately from iCloud progress.

Before uninstalling, remember that local progress may be lost. If you enabled iCloud, verify your progress on another device first. We cannot promise recovery of unsynced local data.

Purchases and restoration

Apple processes in-app purchases. To restore eligible non-consumable purchases such as Remove Ads, open the shop and choose Restore Purchases while signed in with the purchasing Apple account. Consumable coin packs are not restored in the same way.

For billing or refund requests, use Apple’s purchase support. For a missing in-game benefit, contact us with the product name and the time of the purchase. Do not send payment card details or account passwords.

Something still looks wrong?

Update the app, close and reopen it, and check your internet connection for ads or purchases. If a puzzle or animation gets stuck, note the world and level before replaying.

Include the level number, device model, iOS version, app version, and steps that reproduce the issue. Avoid sharing personal information in screenshots.
