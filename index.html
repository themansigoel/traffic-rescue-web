A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT WONDER

Good questions.
Clear answers.

What is Traffic Rescue?

Traffic Rescue is an iOS car puzzle game with five worlds and 64 levels. Move vehicles in the right order, work around obstacles, and open a route through the city.

How do School Zone levels work?

Clear the other traffic first. Tap the bus to visit the next numbered stop. Children board automatically, and the bus waits for the next tap. After the final pickup, one last tap takes everyone to school.

How many hints are free?

Each level includes two free hints. Replaying does not refill them. After that, you can choose to watch a rewarded ad for one extra hint. No coins are deducted.

Does the game show ads?

Yes. An interstitial may appear after every third completed level. The Remove Ads purchase disables interstitials. Rewarded ads for extra hints remain optional.

How is progress saved?

Progress is saved on your device. iCloud synchronization starts only when you choose Sync in Settings. Propagation between devices can take time.

Is analytics required?

No. Share gameplay analytics is off by default. You can choose to enable or disable it in the game settings.
